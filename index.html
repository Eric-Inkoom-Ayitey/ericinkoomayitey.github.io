About

I am a QA & Systems Implementation Engineer and Data Scientist currently at Devnest Systems, where I spearhead end-to-end quality assurance strategies, spanning manual testing, Playwright automation, security, performance, and full product on-boarding for enterprise clients.

I bring a systems-thinking approach to every engagement, ensuring software not only works, but works well.

When I'm not writing test scripts or building dashboards, you'll find me volunteering at tech outreach programs, mentoring the next generation of data professionals, or competing at AI hackathons.
